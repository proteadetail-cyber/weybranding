WEY / WEBSITE DESIGN & DEVELOPMENT

Website work and supporting design

The projects collected here show our website focus alongside selected branding and visual design work. Ubunye Safety & Compliance is a WEY website client, with a link to the live site so you can explore the actual experience. The design archive includes identity and packaging projects; those pieces show another side of the visual thinking that can support a website.

When planning your own project, think about the full journey rather than only the first screen. Visitors need to understand the offer, find relevant information and know how to take the next step. The structure of the pages, the clarity of the content and the behaviour of the interface all play a part. Animation can give that journey character when it is considered alongside the purpose of each section.

Bring a description of your business and its audience when you contact us, together with any required pages or features. Reference projects can help communicate your preferences, particularly when you explain what attracts you to them. Explore our Services page for the areas we work in and our Process page for the steps from brief to launch. The estimate calculator provides a starting point for a landing page or custom website enquiry.

Choose a useful reference

Each project is labelled by its role. Ubunye is live website work; the other entries include identity, packaging and store concepts. They are not website templates. When saving a reference, note what you want to carry into your own project—tone, content structure, product presentation or interaction—and what needs to change for your audience. That gives a design conversation something concrete to work from.

A website brief usually starts with the pages visitors need, the information they must understand and the actions the site should support. Those decisions shape the visual system and help us judge whether an animation or feature is useful.
