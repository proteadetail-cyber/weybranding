05
PLANNING / WEBSITE ESTIMATE

Outline a website scope.

The estimate calculator lets visitors choose a landing page or custom website and add the listed features to see an itemised total. It prepares an email in the visitor’s own email app; nothing is sent until they choose to send it. This gives a business owner a way to gather a rough scope before speaking with WEY. The amount is a starting estimate, not a fixed proposal: the final quote depends on the agreed pages, content and integrations. Hosting, domains and paid provider fees are separate.
