WEY / WEBSITE DESIGN & DEVELOPMENT

What to include in your website enquiry

A useful brief does not need to be long. Begin with a description of your business, the people you want to reach and the main action you want visitors to take. Tell us whether you need a new website, a redesign or a focused landing page. If you already have a site, include its address and explain what is working and what you want to improve. This gives the conversation a practical starting point.

List the pages or sections you expect to need and any features that are important to your business. These might include an enquiry form, editable content, booking integration, a project showcase or an online store. Let us know which content and brand assets you already have. Reference websites can help explain your visual preferences, but a short note about what you like in each reference is more useful than a link on its own.

Share your preferred timing and any budget context you are comfortable providing. The website calculator offers an itemised estimate for the listed build options, including a landing page and a custom website with additional features. Final scope and tax treatment are confirmed separately. The enquiry form prepares a message for your email app rather than sending it automatically. You can review and edit that message before sending, or contact hello@weybrand.co.za directly. Explore our work and process if you would like more context before getting in touch.
