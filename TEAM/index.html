WEY / COLLABORATION

How the disciplines meet in a website

Strategy frames the business question and the audience. Design sets the information hierarchy, typography, imagery and interaction states. Development turns those decisions into a responsive website and makes sure each feature behaves as intended. These disciplines overlap: a change to content can affect the layout, and a technical constraint can change how an animation should work.

Your knowledge of the business is part of the process. You know the offer, the questions customers ask and the details that must be accurate. Sharing those facts early and reviewing the work against the agreed goals keeps the design grounded. WEY brings the strategy, design and development decisions together around the scope of each project, so the finished site feels like one coherent experience.

That collaboration applies whether the brief is a single landing page or a larger website. The number of pages and features changes, but the basic questions stay useful: who is the site for, what do they need to understand and what should they be able to do next?

What each part contributes

Strategy turns a broad business goal into decisions about audience, content and page priorities. It helps identify what belongs on the site and what a visitor should be able to do. Design makes those priorities visible through layout, typography, imagery and interaction. Development builds the responsive pages and connects the agreed functionality so the experience works across common screen sizes.

The three disciplines inform one another throughout a project. A change to the offer can affect the page structure; new content can change the layout; and an integration may affect how a feature behaves. Reviewing work at the right points helps catch those changes while there is still room to make a considered decision. The goal is a website that feels cohesive because its message, design and build have been developed together.

Clients are part of that working relationship. Your knowledge of the business, timely feedback and accurate content help shape the result. WEY brings the creative and technical direction, explains decisions and keeps the agreed scope in view. The way people contribute can vary with a project’s needs, but clear communication and shared expectations remain useful from the first conversation through launch.
