07 / STUDIO NOTES

Look a little closer.

01 / IDENTITY

A logo is the start, not the system.

+

An identity needs to work when the logo is small, absent or sitting beside everything else.

Typography, spacing, colour and image direction create recognition across a brand. If those choices are inconsistent, a strong logo has to do too much work.

When reviewing an identity, look at a plain document, a social post and a mobile screen. The same point of view should survive in all three.

02 / PROCESS

What a useful brief actually contains.

+

Good briefs explain the problem before they suggest the answer.

Start with the business, the people you want to reach and what you want them to understand or do. Include where the work will appear and the constraints it needs to respect.

Visual references help when you explain why you chose them. “I like the typography” gives a designer more to work with than “make it like this.”

03 / DIGITAL

Motion should help you understand.

+

A transition can explain where you are going and how to get back.

Movement works best when it connects two states. A page growing out of a selected pillar shows the relationship between the navigation and the destination.

The motion should finish cleanly and respect reduced-motion settings. Once it has done its job, it should let you get on with the page.
