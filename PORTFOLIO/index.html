WEY / WEBSITE DESIGN & DEVELOPMENT

Look beyond the first screen

The portfolio brings together website work and selected branding projects. For a live website, the experience extends beyond its opening image: page structure, useful content and the route to an enquiry all affect how people use it. Ubunye Safety & Compliance is linked here as a live website client. The other selected projects show visual identity, packaging and design work that can inform the broader look and feel of a brand.

When reviewing a website reference, consider both what attracts your attention and what helps you understand the business. Typography can set the tone, while spacing and hierarchy make content easier to navigate. Animation can introduce an idea or guide a transition, but the page still needs to communicate its purpose clearly. Responsive development brings those choices together across phone, tablet and desktop screens.

If a particular project or visual direction interests you, include it when you contact us. Explain which elements are relevant to your own business and which requirements are different. Your website might need fewer pages, a more direct lead-generation journey or additional content tools. Our Services and Process pages describe how we approach those decisions. You can also use the estimate calculator to choose a website type and explore listed features. It gives you a starting point for discussing scope rather than replacing the project conversation.
