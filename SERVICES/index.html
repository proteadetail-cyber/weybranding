WEY / WEBSITE DESIGN & DEVELOPMENT

Choose the build that suits your business

A landing page and a larger website serve different purposes. A landing page concentrates one message into a single page, with a clear action for the visitor. A custom website gives you room to explain multiple services, organise content and build a more detailed journey. Before choosing features, it helps to decide what information visitors need and what they should be able to do after reading it.

Additional features should support that purpose. Editable content is useful when your information changes regularly. A blog or updates section gives recurring content a home. Booking integration can help organise consultations, while an online store introduces products and checkout requirements. We consider these features as part of the overall structure and visual direction, with responsive layouts and motion supporting the experience.

Our public calculator lists defined build options and their estimate prices. A landing page is R1,500.00, and the custom website base starts at R4,500.00. Hosting, domains, provider fees and paid subscriptions are separate from the listed build cost. Larger or unusual integrations need a scope discussion so that expectations are clear. Share your required pages, available content and priorities when you enquire; we can use that information to agree what is included before a formal quote.
