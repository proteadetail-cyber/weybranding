05 / Branding & Identity

Beetle Juice

An online vape store in Middelburg, South Africa. Inspired by one of the owner's favourite cars — a Beetle.

Year
2024
Timeframe
4 days
Tools
Procreate
Category
Branding & Identity

The Problem

The old-fashioned way of vaping (coil and juice) is much safer than cigarettes — but disposables aren't healthy at all. Most online stores lean into the disposable industry, even when there's a better alternative.

The Solution

Beetle Juice produces its own line of vape juices to influence people to switch from the disposable industry back to the original way of vaping.

A bold, high-contrast identity built for an online vape store to stand out at a glance — drawing from the owner's favourite car and the industry they're trading in.
